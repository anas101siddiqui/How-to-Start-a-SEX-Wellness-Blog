## ⚖️ Step 1: Understand the Legal Requirements Before You Start
 
Before you register a domain or purchase hosting, you must understand the legal landscape surrounding adult content publishing. This step is non-negotiable, and skipping it can lead to serious legal consequences.
 
**Key legal considerations for adult bloggers:**
 
✔ **Age Verification Compliance:** A growing number of countries and regions now require adult websites to confirm that visitors are at least 18 years old. The United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Australia, and many US states have introduced age verification rules, and enforcement is getting stricter. These laws mainly target websites that host pornographic or sexually explicit material, so an educational wellness blog may be treated differently, but you should never assume. Add a clear age gate or 18+ disclaimer page that visitors must acknowledge before accessing any content, and check the rules in the countries where most of your readers live.
 
✔ **Content Ownership and Copyright:** Only publish content that you own or have explicit rights to publish. Never use images, videos, or text from other adult websites without proper licensing or permission. Use royalty-free stock sites or create original content.
 
✔ **Privacy Policy and Terms of Service:** Your blog must have a clear Privacy Policy that outlines how user data is collected and used, as well as comprehensive Terms of Service that define what content is permissible on your platform. These are also important signals for search engines and payment processors.
 
✔ **DMCA Compliance:** Understand how the DMCA works and use a DMCA-protected host to safeguard your original content. If someone steals your content, having DMCA protections in place allows you to issue takedown notices quickly. At the same time, never treat hosting protections as an excuse to publish material you do not have the rights to.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Laws change often and differ from country to country. This guide is for education only and is not legal advice, so consider speaking with a qualified lawyer before you launch.

## 🔗 Step 5: Point Your Domain to QloudHost (DNS Configuration)
 
After purchasing your domain from NameCheap and your hosting from QloudHost, you need to connect them by updating your domain’s DNS (Domain Name System) records. This tells the internet where to find your website when someone types your domain name into a browser.
 
Here is how to do it:
 
1. Log in to your NameCheap account and go to the Domain List section.
2. Find your domain and click “Manage.”
3. Navigate to the “Advanced DNS” tab. You will see the existing DNS records.
4. Look for the A Record with the host set to “@” and change its value to your QloudHost server’s IP address, which you received in the welcome email after purchasing hosting. If NameCheap added default parking page records, replace or delete them so they do not conflict.
5. Add a CNAME record with the host “www” pointing to your domain name (for example, yourdomain.com).
6. Save your changes.
DNS propagation typically takes between 24 and 48 hours, though it often happens much faster. During this time, your domain is being updated across global DNS servers. You can use a tool like whatsmydns.net to check the propagation status.
 
> [!TIP]
> 💡 **Quick Check:** If your site does not load after a day or two, confirm that the A record matches the IP address from your QloudHost welcome email.
